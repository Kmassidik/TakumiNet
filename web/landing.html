Build a network and watch it work.

A free network lab in your browser. Drop in routers, switches and computers, run a cable between them, then follow every packet from one device to the next.

Wire it up

Pick a category, drag a device onto the canvas, then connect two devices with the right cable: copper straight-through, crossover, serial, fiber or console. Set addresses in the device window, like on real equipment.

Follow the packet

Press Play and each device makes its decision in turn: ARP, DNS, ping, a web page. Open any captured frame to read its layers and its bytes, and filter the list by protocol.

Check your work

Guided lessons give you steps to do, and checks that read the live network. When something fails you see what was expected, what the network did, and a hint.

Free, and yours to change

TakumiNet runs entirely in your browser, so your networks stay on your machine. It needs no account and works offline once it has loaded. The engine, the interface and every picture are original work, released under the MIT licence. Anyone can send a pull request or fork it and build their own version.
