Wire it up

Pick a category, drag a device onto the canvas, then connect two devices with the right cable: copper straight-through, crossover, serial, fiber or console. Set addresses in the device window, like on real equipment.

Follow the packet

Press Play and each device makes its decision in turn: ARP, DNS, ping, a web page. Open any captured frame to read its layers and its bytes, and filter the list by protocol.

Questions people ask

Is Takumi free?

Yes. It is free to use and open source under the MIT licence.

Do I need an account or an install?

No. Open it in your browser and start building. There is nothing to install.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the page, the network engine and the pictures are saved in your browser, so it keeps working without a connection.

Where are my networks saved?

On your own computer. Save a network as an .xml file and open it again later. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I use it to teach a class?

Yes. The guided lessons and ready-made example networks are made for classrooms, and you can share any network as an .xml file.

Free, and yours to change

Takumi runs entirely in your browser, so your networks stay on your machine. It needs no account and works offline once it has loaded. The engine, the interface and every picture are original work, released under the MIT licence. Anyone can send a pull request or fork it and build their own version.
