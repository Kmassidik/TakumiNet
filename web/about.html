About

A network lab that belongs in your browser.

TakumiNet is a clean-room network emulator for learning and teaching. Build a topology, configure real protocol state, follow each packet, and check your work without installing a simulator.

Free to useOpen source · MITNo accountWorks offline

Free, private, and local

No account, sign-in, subscription, or activation is needed. The simulation runs in your browser's WebAssembly engine; each tab has its own workspace and browser-local saves.

After one complete load, the service worker caches the application, templates, art, sounds, and engine for offline use.

Independent and original

TakumiNet's Rust engine, browser code, generated 3D artwork, and synthesized sounds are original to this project. No third-party media or icon libraries are bundled.

Original asset pipeline

How the artwork was made

The visual assets have traceable, reproducible provenance. Image generation used written descriptions only—never vendor artwork, screenshots, or icons as references.

  1. Generate original sheets. Text-only prompts in web/art/3d/CHATGPT.md were supplied to ChatGPT image generation to create the 3D device, toolbar, cable, application, and mascot sheets.
  2. Cut and validate the icons. tools/build_art.py uses tools/slice_sheets.py and the macOS Vision helper tools/cutout.swift to remove backgrounds, split each grid, center square icons, and check that every image is non-empty and correctly framed.
  3. Record the source. web/art/3d/MANIFEST.json records every source sheet title, short SHA-256 digest, output group, icon order, and grid dimensions. The optimized PNGs are committed with the application.

Icons are shipped as WebP. Sound effects were synthesized for TakumiNet by tools/make_sounds.py.
