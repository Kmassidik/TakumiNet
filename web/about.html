About Takumi

A free network lab that runs in your browser. No account, no install. One person builds it, and anyone can change it.

Why it exists

Learning how networks work should not need an account, an installer or a licence key. Takumi opens in a browser tab, works offline after your first visit, and keeps your networks on your own computer.

Who makes it

What is inside

The engine

Written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly, so the whole network runs inside your browser tab.

The interface

Plain HTML, CSS and TypeScript, with the workspace drawn on a canvas. Nothing to install.
